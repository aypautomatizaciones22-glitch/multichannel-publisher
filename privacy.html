# Privacy Policy **Effective date:** November 20, 2026 ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how MultiChannel Publisher Automation handles information when users authorize and use the application. The application is designed to automate the distribution of video content to social media accounts connected by their owners. ## 2. Information We Access Depending on the permissions granted by the user and the connected platform, the application may access: - Basic account information, such as account identifiers and profile information. - Authorization tokens required to connect to supported social media platforms. - Video files, titles, descriptions, and other content supplied for publication. - Publication status and technical responses returned by the platforms. The application only requests information necessary to operate its intended features. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information is used exclusively to: - Connect authorized social media accounts. - Generate videos from scripts supplied by the content owner through Fliki's API. - Transfer and publish videos to the social media accounts authorized by the user. - Monitor publication status and identify technical errors. - Maintain and troubleshoot the automation workflow. ## 4. Video Processing and Third-Party Services The automation uses n8n, operated locally by the application owner, to coordinate the workflow. Video generation is performed through Fliki's API. Generated video files are downloaded and processed by the automation before being distributed to the social media platforms selected by the account owner. Supported platforms may include YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Each third-party service processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms. ## 5. Data Storage and Security The automation is operated in a local n8n environment. Access tokens and configuration information are maintained within the automation environment and are used to perform authorized operations. Reasonable technical measures are applied to protect account credentials and content from unauthorized access. ## 6. Data Sharing The application does not sell users' personal information. Information and content are transmitted only to the third-party services necessary to perform the functions authorized by the account owner. ## 7. Data Retention and Deletion Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the automation. Users may revoke the application's access through the settings of the relevant social media platform. Users may also request the deletion of information associated with the application by contacting the application operator. ## 8. User Rights and Choices Users remain in control of their social media accounts and may revoke authorization at any time through the relevant platform. Users may contact the application operator with questions or requests concerning their information. ## 9. Children's Privacy This application is not intended to collect personal information from children. ## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will be published on the official website. ## 11. Contact For privacy-related questions or requests, contact: **Email: aypautomatizaciones22@gmail.com
